How it works

You see what we see, before you pay a cent

Most shop bills are a surprise because the customer never saw the car. Ours come with photos, measurements and a price you approve line by line.

  1. 1

    Book online or call

    Pick a slot that suits you; most weekdays have same-day space. Drop the keys in the box if you're early.

  2. 2

    We inspect, with photos

    Your technician texts a link: photos and measurements of everything worn, rated urgent, soon or fine.

  3. 3

    Approve the quote

    Tick what you want done, skip what can wait. The price you approve is the price you pay.

  4. 4

    Drive away covered

    Old parts in a bag if you want them, a 24-month warranty on the new ones, and a free exterior wash.

Why Torque & Co.

A shop that would rather lose a job than oversell one

Marisol Reyes took over her father's two-bay garage in 2011. It is nine bays now, and the rules on the wall have not changed.

  • We show you the worn partOn your phone during the job, and in your hand afterwards if you ask.
  • Nothing is done without a yesIf we find more mid-job, the car waits while we call. No "while we were in there".
  • OEM-quality parts, never mystery brandsEvery part on the invoice has a brand and a part number you can look up.
  • Techs are salaried, not on commissionNobody here earns more by finding more wrong with your car.
