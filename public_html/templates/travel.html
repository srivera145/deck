Featured journey · 12 days

Japan in the slow season

Late autumn, when the maples turn and the crowds go home. Tokyo to the Seto Inland Sea by rail, with two nights in a ryokan and a whole morning with nothing planned at all.

Small group · max 10 Moderate pace From $6,850 pp
  1. 1
    Days 1–3 · Tokyo

    Arrive, and walk it off

    Private transfer from Haneda. A morning at Tsukiji with a chef who still buys there, an afternoon in Yanaka's old lanes.

  2. 2
    Days 4–5 · Hakone

    Ryokan and hot springs

    Two nights at a family-run ryokan with a private onsen. Kaiseki dinner both evenings; Fuji, if it decides to appear.

  3. 3
    Days 6–9 · Kyoto

    Temples before the gates open

    Early access to Tōfuku-ji for the maples, a tea ceremony in a Gion townhouse, and a free day with a bicycle.

  4. 4
    Days 10–11 · Naoshima

    Art islands of the Inland Sea

    Ferry out to the Benesse museums and Teshima's single-room gallery. Sunset from the pier, yellow pumpkin optional.

  5. 5
    Day 12 · Osaka

    One last dinner, then home

    Street food in Dōtonbori with your guide, then a transfer to Kansai for the overnight flight.

  • 11 nights, boutique hotels & ryokan
  • 7-day JR pass and all transfers
  • 11 breakfasts, 6 dinners
  • Local guide in every city
