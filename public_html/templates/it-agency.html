// faq

Questions IT managers ask us first.

Something else?

Ask an engineer directly. You will get an answer, not a calendar link.

Ask a question
We already have an IT person. Do we still need you?
Usually yes, and they are usually relieved. We take the 2am pages, the patching and the audits, and your IT person gets to work on the projects they were hired for. About half our clients keep an in-house lead who works alongside our team.
How long does onboarding take?
Six weeks for a typical 100-person company, with the helpdesk live from the end of week one. Larger estates or a cloud migration add time, and the plan you approve in week two says exactly how much.
What counts as a P1, and what happens when one is raised?
Anything that stops a team working or exposes data: an outage, a ransomware alert, a payroll system down on payday. A named engineer acknowledges within 15 minutes, opens a bridge, and posts updates every 30 minutes until it is resolved.
Are we locked into a contract?
No. Every plan is month to month with 30 days’ notice, annual billing included. When you leave we hand over credentials, documentation and the infrastructure code, and run a handover call with whoever takes over.
Do you resell cloud with a markup?
No. Cloud and licences are billed at cost, in your own accounts, so you can see every line. We are paid for the engineering, not the consumption.
Can you work with our existing software vendor?
Yes. We regularly sit between a client and their ERP, CRM or line-of-business vendor, and we will join their support calls so you do not have to translate.
