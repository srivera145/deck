How we work

Sixteen weeks from question to running change.

A typical strategy engagement. Operations and integration work runs longer, but the shape is the same.

  1. Weeks 1–3

    Diagnose

    Build the fact base with your numbers, not ours. Interview the top forty people and the customers who left.

    • Value map and baseline
    • Three questions worth answering
  2. Weeks 4–8

    Decide

    Options with real trade-offs, tested with your executive team in working sessions rather than read-outs.

    • Board-ready recommendation
    • Financial case, by quarter
  3. Weeks 9–14

    Design

    Turn the decision into owners, budgets and a ninety-day plan. The plan is written by your managers, with us.

    • Initiative charters and KPIs
    • Transformation office set-up
  4. Week 15 onward

    Deliver

    We stay through the first results, then step back. Fees from here are tied to the value the plan promised.

    • Monthly value tracking
    • A team that runs it without us
