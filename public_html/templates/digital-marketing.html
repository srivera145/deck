Do you lock clients into long contracts?
The first term is 90 days, because that is how long it takes to judge paid media fairly. After that it is rolling monthly with 30 days' notice. Our longest client has been with us since 2017 without a contract renewal.
Who owns the ad accounts and the data?
You do, always. We work inside your accounts with user access you can revoke at any time. If we part ways you keep every campaign, audience, creative file and dashboard.
How soon will we see results?
Paid channels usually show a clear signal in four to six weeks and a fair read at 90 days. SEO takes four to six months to move meaningfully. We put the 90-day target in writing in week two.
What is the minimum ad spend?
Around $10k a month across all channels. Below that, a retainer eats too much of the budget and we will tell you so, along with what we would do in your shoes.
Do you work with B2B and SaaS?
Yes, about a third of our clients. We connect LinkedIn, Google and review sites to HubSpot or Salesforce so pipeline and revenue, not form fills, are the number we report.
How do you report?
A one-page memo every Monday (what happened, why, what we are changing), a live dashboard you can open any time, and a monthly review call. No forty-slide decks.
