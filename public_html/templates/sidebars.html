OAuth providers

Let people sign in with an account they already have. Harbor ships GitHub, Google and generic OpenID Connect; each one is four lines of config.

auth:
  providers:
    - github:
        client_id: ${GITHUB_CLIENT_ID}

Callback URLs match exactly

Including the scheme and any trailing slash. Most failed sign-ins are this.
